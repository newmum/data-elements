# Third-party database brand artwork

The connection picker uses locally bundled SVG brand artwork from Devicon and Simple Icons. These are upstream community-maintained depictions of the brands, not a claim that every file is an official vendor-distributed asset. They identify database types only; no affiliation, sponsorship, or endorsement is implied. No icon is fetched from an external server at runtime.

## Devicon

Eight SVGs (MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQL Server, Oracle, SQLite, MariaDB, MongoDB, Redis) are unmodified copies from Devicon v2.17.0. They retain the upstream colors, paths, and square viewBoxes. Oracle is the complete red wordmark, not a fabricated letter icon. Its wide aspect ratio naturally makes it finer at small sizes.

## Simple Icons / Snowflake

- [Simple Icons 15.14.0 license](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/15.14.0/LICENSE.md): CC0 1.0 Universal. A copy is at `src/assets/database/LICENSE-SIMPLE-ICONS.md`.
- [Pinned source/color metadata](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/15.14.0/data/simple-icons.json) gives Snowflake color `#29B5E8` and identifies `https://www.snowflake.com/brand-guidelines/` as its source and brand-guidelines URL.
- The only local change is setting that brand color on the SVG root. The paths and registered-trademark symbol are unchanged.
- [Simple Icons legal disclaimer](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/15.14.0/DISCLAIMER.md) explicitly says its CC0 release does not imply every underlying logo is CC0, and users must consider underlying brand rights and permissions. Its Snowflake metadata has no separate logo-license grant.
- The official Snowflake brand-center page could not be inspected because it returned HTTP 403. No vendor permission or unrestricted commercial logo reuse is claimed. Review applicable vendor brand policies before public redistribution beyond this database-identification prototype.
